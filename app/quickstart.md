---
title: "Quickstart"
description: From install to a working cart widget and your first tracked cart in about ten minutes.
---

Five steps. At the end, shoppers see their Cart ID on your cart page, and you see their cart in
the Dashboard.

## 1. Install and choose a plan

Install CB Customer Cart Manager from the Shopify App Store and approve the permissions.

Shopify then asks you to choose a plan. You must pick one, even Free, before the app opens. Cart
tracking starts on **Basic**; on Free, cart tracking is off, so the steps below need a paid plan
or its trial to show a Cart ID. Paid plans
include a 3-day free trial. See [Plans and billing](/app/guides/plans-and-billing).

You land on **Home**, where the **Setup Guide** tracks the three remaining steps.

## 2. Enable the app embed

In the Setup Guide, step **Initial Set Up**, click **Enable App Embed**. Shopify's theme editor
opens with **Cb Customer Cart Widget** switched on under **App embeds**. Click **Save** in the
theme editor.

Back in the app, the step shows **Activated** within a few seconds. Details:
[Enable the app embed](/app/setup/enable-app-embed).

## 3. Pick a widget design

Step **Modify Cart Widget** → **Go to Templates**. Choose one of the nine ready-made templates, or
click **✦ Create Custom Design** to build your own, then **Save Template**. See
[Choose a widget template](/app/setup/widget-templates).

On Free, only **Classic Elegance** is available.

## 4. Check your settings

Step **Configure App Settings** → **Go to Settings**. The defaults work, but look at two things:

- **Widget Visibility → Widget Pages.** By default the widget shows on the **Cart Page** only
  (plus the cart drawer). See [Widget visibility](/app/setup/widget-visibility).
- **Popup & Support → Support Form Configuration.** By default shoppers fill in one field,
  "Query", before sending their cart. See [Popup and support form](/app/setup/popup-and-support-form).

Click **Save** in the bar at the top of the page.

## 5. Test it on your storefront

Open your store in a private window, add a product to the cart and open the cart page. You should
see **Your Cart ID: …** and the support button.

Now open **Dashboard** in the app. Your cart appears under **Live Carts** within a few seconds;
the page refreshes on its own.

> **That is the whole loop.** Shoppers can now quote their Cart ID or press the button, and you
> can open their exact cart, log the contact and turn it into a draft order.
