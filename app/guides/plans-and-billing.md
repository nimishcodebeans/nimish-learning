---
title: "Plans and billing"
description: How plans, trials, limits and plan changes work.
---

Cart Manager is billed through Shopify. Charges appear on your Shopify invoice.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Free trial |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | $0 | – |
| **Basic** | $8.99 / 30 days | 3 days |
| **Advanced** | $37.99 / 30 days | 3 days |
| **Enterprise** | $87.99 / 30 days | 3 days |

Prices in USD. For what each plan includes, see [Plan features](/app/reference/plan-features).

You must choose a plan, Free included, before the app opens. If there is no active plan (for
example after reinstalling), the app takes you to Shopify's plan page first.

## See your usage

**Home → Plan Usage & Caps** shows your plan and three meters:

| Meter | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Cart Tracking Cap** | Carts tracked this calendar month out of your limit. The bar turns red at the limit. |
| **Draft Order Cap** | Draft orders created from the app this month, or **Unlimited** |
| **History Retention** | How many days carts are kept |

Monthly counts reset on the 1st of each month.

## What happens at a limit

- **Tracked carts.** New carts stop being tracked, and the storefront widget does not show for
  them. Carts you already track keep updating. The Dashboard shows **Monthly Cart Tracking Limit
  Reached**.
- **Draft orders.** You cannot create more draft orders from the app this month. **Monthly Limit
  Reached** appears on the Draft Orders page.

Nothing is deleted when you reach a limit.

## History retention

Carts are kept for your plan's retention period (14, 60 or 90 days), counted from their last
activity, and then removed from the app. Follow up on, or convert, any cart you need before then.
Upgrading gives you a longer period from then on.

## Change your plan

Click **Upgrade Plan** on Home or on any 🔒 locked feature. The **Plan Upgrade Required** window
shows the plan you need; click **Upgrade Plan now →** to go to Shopify's plan page and confirm.

Downgrades are also made on Shopify's plan page. After a downgrade, features your new plan does
not include switch off. For example, on Free the popup, exit-intent and support form stop showing
on your storefront, and cart tracking stops.

## Uninstalling

Uninstalling cancels your subscription through Shopify. The widget disappears from your storefront
straight away. If you reinstall, choose a plan again.
