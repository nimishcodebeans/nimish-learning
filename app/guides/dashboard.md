# Use the dashboard

**Dashboard** is where you see every cart. It refreshes itself every few seconds, and again as
soon as you come back to the browser tab, so you can leave it open.

## The numbers at the top

| Card | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Today's Visitors** | Visitors since midnight, split into **New** and **Returning**. The **LIVE** count is visitors active in the last 5 minutes. |
| **Today's Carts** | Carts with activity today. The **LIVE** count is carts active in the last 30 minutes. |
| **Total cart value** | The combined value of all carts in the app (archived carts excluded) |
| **Total Sales** | The value of orders placed from tracked carts, with the number of orders |

Exact definitions: [Cart statuses and metrics](../reference/statuses-and-metrics.md).

## Tabs

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All Carts** | Every session, including visitors who only browsed |
| **Live Carts** | Carts active in the last 30 minutes. These shoppers are on your store now. |
| **Today's Carts** | Carts with activity since midnight |
| **Week to Date Carts** | Carts active in the last 7 days |
| **Abandoned Carts** | No activity for over an hour, and no checkout or order |
| **Support Tickets** | Shoppers who pressed the widget button. See [Handle support tickets](support-tickets.md). |
| **Archived** | Carts you hid with **Hide Cart** |

Each tab shows its count in brackets.

## Search

The search box finds carts by **Cart ID** (either format), customer **email** or **name**. On the
Support Tickets tab it also searches the ticket message.

## The cart table

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Cart ID** | The ID, and a status badge: **✓ Order Placed**, **Cart**, **Checkout** or **Browsing** |
| **Customer** | Email or name, or **Guest** |
| **Company** | For B2B customers, or the company on the address |
| **Cart Value** | The cart total |
| **Creation Time** / **Last Updated Time** | When the cart started and when the shopper last did something |
| **Cart Details** | **View Details**, plus icons for an order placed and the latest contact |

The contact icon's colour tells you who reached out last:

- green: **Customer contacted us**
- amber: **Support Request**
- blue: **We contacted customer**

Choose 10, 25 or 50 **Rows per page** at the bottom.

## Keep the list tidy

Open a cart and click **Hide Cart** to move it to **Archived**, for example a test cart or a
cart you have finished with. Archived carts leave every other tab and stop counting towards
**Total cart value**. Click **Unarchive** in the Archived tab to bring one back.

## If you see a red banner

- **Cart Tracking Disabled.** You are on the Free plan, which does not track carts.
- **Monthly Cart Tracking Limit Reached.** You have used this month's tracked carts. New carts are
  not tracked until the 1st of next month or until you upgrade. Carts you already track keep
  updating.

Both banners have an **Upgrade Plan** button. See [Plans and billing](plans-and-billing.md).
