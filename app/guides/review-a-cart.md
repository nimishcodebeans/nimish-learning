---
title: "Review a cart"
description: Everything on the cart page, and what you can do from it.
---

From the Dashboard, click **View Details** on any cart. The page title is **Cart ID: …** with the
cart's status beside it. It refreshes itself every few seconds.

## Items

Every product in the cart: image, title (links to the product in Shopify), variant, any line-item
properties, price × quantity and line total. The **Total** is at the bottom, next to the
[draft order buttons](/app/guides/draft-orders).

## Activity Feed

A timeline of what the shopper did, newest first:

- Home page viewed, Collection viewed, Page viewed
- Product viewed
- Product added to cart, Product removed from cart
- Cart viewed
- Checkout started, Checkout shipping submitted, Payment info submitted
- Order completed

Hover a product to see its image. Click **View Activity History** under Visitor Information for
the full history on its own page.

The Activity Feed needs **Basic** or above. On Free it is blurred.

## Visitor Information

City and country, browser, operating system and IP address, worked out from the shopper's
connection.

## Customer

If the shopper is signed in or gave their email at checkout, their details appear here: name,
email, phone, shipping address (with **View map**) and billing address.

If not, you can attach a customer yourself:

- **Add customer** searches your Shopify customers. Click one to attach it.
- **Create Customer** adds a new customer to Shopify: **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**
  (required) and **Phone** (with country code, for example `+919876543210`).
- The **⋯** menu has **Change customer** and **Remove Customer**.

The customer you attach is used when you [create a draft order](/app/guides/draft-orders).

## Log a contact

Keep a record of every conversation with the shopper.

1. Click **Mark Contact** at the top (or **Add Contact Log** under Contact History).
2. Choose the **Contact Type**: **Customer contacted us** or **We contacted customer**.
3. Add **Notes** if you like, and click **Save Contact Log**.

On a cart that has not reached checkout, the **Reviewing Cart** banner offers the same two
shortcuts. Your logs appear under **Contact History**, and the latest shows as an icon in the
Dashboard.

## Notes & Instructions and Cart Attributes

- **Notes & Instructions:** a free-text note about the customer or order.
- **Cart Attributes:** key and value pairs, for example `gift_message` = `Happy birthday!`.
  Click **Add custom attribute** to add one. Editing attributes needs **Advanced** or above.

Save with the bar at the top of the page. Both are copied into any draft order you create from the
cart.

> **Heads up.** Saving Cart Attributes here replaces the attributes the cart had from the
> storefront.

## Support Tickets and Conversion Summary

- **Support Tickets** lists the shopper's latest requests from the widget. **View all** opens
  them in the Dashboard.
- **Conversion Summary** tells you what became of the cart: **Order Converted** with a link to the
  order, or **Draft Order Generated** with a link to the draft.

## Hide the cart

**Hide Cart** moves the cart to the Dashboard's **Archived** tab. You can still open it there.
