# Handle support tickets

A support ticket is created every time a shopper presses the button on the
[cart widget](../setup/widget-templates.md) and, if you use one, fills in the
[support form](../setup/popup-and-support-form.md). The ticket is attached to their cart, so you
see what they want to buy along with what they asked.

## Where tickets appear

- **Dashboard → Support Tickets.** One row per ticket: **Cart ID / Customer**, **Message** (every
  form field), **Submitted At** and **View Cart**.
- **The cart page → Support Tickets.** The latest requests for that cart.
- **Your inbox,** if you turned on [merchant alerts](../setup/email-notifications.md).

Search the Support Tickets tab by Cart ID, email, name or any word in the message.

## A routine that works

1. **Get the alert.** Turn on **New support ticket submitted by customer** so you never have to
   check.
2. **Open the cart.** From the email, search the Cart ID in the Dashboard; or open
   **Support Tickets** and click **View Cart**.
3. **Read the context.** The items tell you what they want. The Activity Feed tells you what they
   looked at and where they stopped.
4. **Reply.** Answer by email or phone, then click **Mark Contact → We contacted customer** and
   add a note, so your team knows it is handled.
5. **Close the sale.** If they are ready to buy, [create a draft order](draft-orders.md) and send
   the invoice from Shopify.

Turn on the [confirmation email](../setup/email-notifications.md) so shoppers know their request
arrived.

## When a shopper quotes a Cart ID

Shoppers can also read their Cart ID from the widget and send it to you by chat, email or phone.
Paste it into the Dashboard search to open their cart. If IDs are hard to read out, switch to
[Cart Number](../setup/cart-id-format.md) format (`#1024`).
