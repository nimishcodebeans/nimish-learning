# Read your analytics

**Analytics** needs the **Advanced** plan or above. On lower plans the charts are blurred.

## Pick a date range

Click the date button at the top. **Quick Select** offers **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7
days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **Last 365 days** and **Custom**. For a custom range,
pick two dates in the calendar and click **Apply**.

The page opens on **Today**. A single day is shown in 3-hour blocks; longer ranges are shown by
day. Analytics currently covers up to the last 60 days of carts.

## The charts

| Card | What it shows | Use it to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Total carts value** | The combined value of carts in the range | See how much shoppers put in their carts |
| **Total carts count** | Carts with at least one item | Spot busy days and quiet ones |
| **Average cart value** | Total value ÷ number of carts | Test whether bundles or upsells raise basket size |
| **Draft Orders created from the app** | Carts you turned into draft orders | Track how much your team recovers by hand |
| **Top products in carts** | The five products added most, by quantity | Find what people want but may not buy |
| **Customers by location** | The five countries with most carts | Decide on shipping offers or currencies |

Carts are counted on the day of their **last activity**, so a cart started on Monday and updated
on Tuesday counts on Tuesday.

## Questions worth asking

- **Top products in carts** vs your best sellers: a product that is often added but rarely bought
  may have a price, shipping or stock problem.
- **Average cart value** before and after a promotion.
- **Draft Orders created from the app** week by week: is your team following up?
