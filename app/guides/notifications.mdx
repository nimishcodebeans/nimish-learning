CB Customer Cart Manager notifies you and the shopper by email whenever a shopper submits the cart widget. This page explains who gets notified, how to switch alerts on in a few clicks, and what to check when an email does not arrive.

## Who gets notified

| When | Who | Email | Setting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A shopper submits the cart widget | You (the merchant) | **New Support Request** | **Merchant Alerts → New support ticket submitted by customer** |
| A shopper submits the cart widget | The shopper | **Ticket Confirmation** | **Customer Alerts → Support ticket submitted (confirmation email)** |

<Note>
Both alerts are **off** after install. Nothing is sent until you turn them on.
</Note>

## Turn on notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Email Notifications">
    Open **Email Notifications** in the app menu, or go to **Settings → Notifications → Email Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Alert yourself">
    On the **Merchant Alerts** tab, tick **New support ticket submitted by customer**. In **Notification Email**, enter where alerts should go. Separate several addresses with commas: the first one receives the email and the rest are copied in. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm to the shopper">
    On the **Customer Alerts** tab, tick **Support ticket submitted (confirmation email)** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test">
    Open a template, enter a **Recipient Email** under **Test Email** and click **Send Test Email**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Guests have no customer account email. Add an **Email** field to your [support form](/app/setup/popup-and-support-form) so the shopper confirmation can be delivered.
</Tip>

## Customize what is sent

- **Templates:** edit the subject and HTML content of each email, add [Liquid variables](/app/reference/email-variables) and a logo.
- **Sender:** emails go through **CB SMTP Server (Default)**. Choose **Use Custom SMTP Server** on the **SMTP Setup** tab to send from your own domain.

Full details: [Email notifications](/app/setup/email-notifications).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I'm not getting ticket emails">
    - Check that **New support ticket submitted by customer** is ticked and saved under **Merchant Alerts**.
    - Check **Notification Email** for typos.
    - Look in your spam folder, then send yourself a test email from the template editor.
    - With a custom SMTP server, check your server details with a test email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The shopper didn't get a confirmation">
    Turn on **Support ticket submitted (confirmation email)** under **Customer Alerts**. For guests, add an **Email** field to your support form.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
