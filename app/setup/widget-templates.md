# Choose a widget template

The template controls how the Cart ID card looks and what it says. Open it from **Home → Setup
Guide → Go to Templates**, from the **CB Cart Widget** card on Home, or from **Settings → General
settings → Modify Cart Widget**. The page is called **Choose Widget Template**.

## Ready-to-use templates

| Template | Style | Button text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Classic Elegance** (default) | A clean, timeless design that fits any brand. | Submit Your Cart to Support |
| **Midnight Modern** | Sleek dark theme for a premium feel. | Get Support Now |
| **Minimalist Border** | Focus on content with subtle outlines. | Contact Support |
| **Playful Pop** | Bright colors and rounded corners. | Chat with Us |
| **Sunset Vibe** | Warm oranges and soft curves. | Connect with Help |
| **Ocean Breeze** | Calm blues and rounded buttons. | Send Help Request |
| **Forest Eco** | Natural greens for eco-conscious brands. | Message Support |
| **Luxury Gold** | Elegant black and gold design. | Request Concierge |
| **Neon Punch** | High-contrast vibrant colors. | Ping Support |

Each card shows a live preview. Click one, then **Save Template**. The active template is marked
**Currently Active**.

On the **Free** plan only Classic Elegance is available; the others show **🔒 Locked**.

## Create a custom design

Available on Basic and above.

1. Click **✦ Create Custom Design**.
2. Fill in the builder. The **Live Preview** on the right shows exactly how it will appear on
   your storefront.

   | Section | Fields |
   | --- | --- |
   | Name | **Template Name** (required), **Description** |
   | 🏷️ Badge Style | Background Color, Text Color |
   | 🃏 Card Appearance | Background Color, Border Radius, **Show Separator Line** |
   | 🔘 Button Style | Background Color, Text Color, Border Radius, Border Style (for example `2px solid #333`) |
   | ✍️ Typography & Content | Heading Color, Body Text Color, **Heading Text**, **Body Text**, **Button Text** |

3. Click **Save Custom Design**. The new template becomes active straight away.

Your designs are listed under **✨ Your Custom Designs**. Click **✕** on one to delete it. If you
delete the active template, the widget goes back to Classic Elegance.

> **Change the wording, not just the colours.** A custom template is the only way to change the
> widget's heading ("Need Assistance with Your Cart?"), body text and button text, for example to
> write them in your store's language.

## What the shopper sees

- A badge: **Your Cart ID: v_yna3sj0b2** (or **#12** with [number format](cart-id-format.md))
- The heading and body text
- The button. What happens when they press it depends on the
  [support form](popup-and-support-form.md).
