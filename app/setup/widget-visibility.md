# Widget visibility

**Settings → Widget Visibility.** Two settings decide where the widget appears. Click **Save** in
the bar at the top when you are done.

## Target Audience

| Option | Who sees the widget |
| --- | --- |
| **Show to Everyone (All Users)** (default) | Every visitor |
| **Show only to Logged-in Customers** | Only shoppers signed in to a customer account |
| **Show only to Guest Users** | Only shoppers who are not signed in |

## Widget Pages

Tick the storefront pages where the widget should appear:

- **All Pages (Everywhere)**
- **Home Page**
- **Product Pages**
- **Collection Pages**
- **Cart Page** (default, the only one ticked on install)
- **Custom Pages**
- **Blogs & Articles**

Ticking a specific page unticks **All Pages**, and ticking **All Pages** unticks the rest.

On any page where the widget is allowed, it appears in the cart page and inside the cart drawer
when the shopper opens it. With the [popup](popup-and-support-form.md) on, it can also appear as a
popup.

> **Tip.** Most stores keep **Cart Page** only. Shoppers look for help when they are reviewing
> their cart, and the cart drawer is covered on every page anyway.
