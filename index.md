CB Customer Cart Manager shows you every shopper's cart as it happens: what they browsed, what
they added, where they stopped, and whether they ordered. Each cart gets a **Cart ID** that the
shopper sees on your storefront, so when they ask for help you can open exactly the cart they are
looking at, and turn it into a Shopify draft order in one click.

Start with whichever describes what you are trying to do.

| Section | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [Merchant guide](/app) | Everything in the app: install the cart widget, track carts, handle support requests, create draft orders, read analytics and manage your plan. |
| [AI connector (MCP)](/mcp) | Connect Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT or any MCP client to your store's cart data and ask questions in plain language. |
